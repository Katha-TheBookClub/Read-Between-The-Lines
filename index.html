Incident Report:
The Last Page

The Afterword, a fictional student literary journal, was preparing to release a special issue featuring a writing challenge.

At the centre of the issue was a confidential manuscript titled “The Last Page.” The document contained an allegation that someone had received access to the writing challenge prompt before it was officially announced.

But there was a problem.

The allegation had not been verified, and publishing it could damage someone's reputation.

Before the manuscript could be released, it mysteriously disappeared.

Now, conflicting accounts, suspicious circumstances, and hidden clues stand between you and the truth.

Your mission is to investigate the evidence, question the available information, identify who hid the manuscript, and uncover the motive behind the disappearance.

Some secrets are hidden in plain sight. Are you paying attention?

What is Read Between the Lines?

It is an interactive mystery-solving game organised by KATHA – The Book Club, where participants investigate a fictional case using clues and evidence.

Do I need to be a detective or an experienced reader?

Not at all! Curiosity, observation, logical thinking, and teamwork are what matter.

Where will the event take place?

At KMEC Campus, Hyderabad. Exact starting-point details will be shared by the organisers.

When is the event?
